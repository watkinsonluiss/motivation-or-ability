# motivation-or-ability
Do files assocaited with the paper "Motivation or ability? Explaining the effect of party polarisation on proximity voting"

This paper makes use of:

European Election Study 2024 (https://www.gesis.org/en/services/finding-and-accessing-data/international-survey-programs/european-election-studies/ees-2024-study)

Chapel-Hill Expert Survey 2024 (https://www.chesdata.eu/2024-chapel-hill-expert-survey-ches)

The do files:

CHES recoding 2024 - motivation or ability = recoding of the CHES 2024 file

EES recoding 2024 - motivation or ability = recoding of the EES 2024 file, for EU election analysis

EES recoding 2024 - motivation or ability (ppk) = obtain respondent level measures of party position knowledge

EES recoding 2024 - motivation or ability (obtain results)
